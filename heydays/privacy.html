heydays

privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Heydays doesn't collect your personal data. There's no account to create, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you add stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

What you add to Heydays

Your events, dates, notes, categories and the photos you choose are stored on your device.

If iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account, Heydays keeps them in sync across your devices using Apple's iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. The developer of Heydays can't see or access it. Apple's privacy policy covers how iCloud stores your data.

Photos

Heydays never has access to your photo library. When you add photos, you choose them in Apple's photo picker and only those photos are copied into the app.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are scheduled on your device, and widgets read your countdowns on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Information we collect

None. Heydays doesn't send any information about you or how you use it to the developer or to anyone else.

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports and usage statistics.

Deleting your data

You can delete events and memories in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. To remove the copy in iCloud, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, and manage your storage.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
